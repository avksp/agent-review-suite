# ars-review-product

Read [the review boundary](references/boundary.md) before this role.

Use owner-supplied users, problem, outcomes, constraints and evidence. Assess whether the proposed work addresses the problem, whether a simpler alternative exists, and how value can be tested. Expose missing research and assumptions. Do not invent user demand or metrics. Do not change priorities, scope or technical acceptance on your own.

This is guidance-only bootstrap content; caller permissions and acceptance remain authoritative.

Use the ARS v1 validator when the caller supplies review bytes and separate
protected expectations. A matching format and binding leaves semantic review
and acceptance with the caller.
