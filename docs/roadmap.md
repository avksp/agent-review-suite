Each future version needs bounded research and review, validation and delivery evidence.
The preceding release SHA becomes known at delivery. The external delivery
process owns DORA metrics and scheduling. Qualified standalone host roles
can proceed while the optional workflow bridge waits.

Future work considers automatic pack selection, reviewer dispatch and
synthesis of findings.
